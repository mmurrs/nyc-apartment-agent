NYC Apartment Agent · Why This Exists

Every incumbent ships alerts and chat to humans. We ship the apartment-hunt endpoint for agents.

Natural-language search is commoditized — every portal added it in 2025–2026. But nobody exposes an agent-callable, per-request, pay-per-call API with structured JSON. That's the category this service occupies.

§ 01

How we're different

The NYC apartment-hunt landscape has seven major categories: rent-stabilized alert systems (RentReboot), speed-focused StreetEasy monitors (LeaseAlert, FirstMover), multi-source scrapers (Pounce), portal AI modes (Zillow AI, Apartments.com Ai, Zumper in ChatGPT), and 47+ GitHub repos of hunters building their own bots. All of them deliver to human inboxes or their own chat UIs.

None expose an agent-callable API with per-request pricing. That's the structural difference.

Why aggregation across competitors is structurally ours: Zillow owns StreetEasy and will never cross-list Craigslist, The Listings Project, or Facebook Marketplace. RentReboot scrapes broadly but ships email summaries to humans. We're the only service that returns structured JSON with multi-source listings, subway walk times, and live Citi Bike availability in one call.

Why transit-native matters: We run transit402.dev, the real-time NYC transit endpoint for agents. Subway walk times are bundled per listing, and Citi Bike dock availability is live (bikes + docks) at query time. No other service has micromobility constraints built in.

Verifiable execution: This service runs on EigenCompute (TEE-attested VM). Every response includes receipts showing exactly what the service paid upstream to answer your query — service name, route, USD amount, transaction hash. Check provenance at verify.eigencloud.xyz/app/0x94D4dF7f4Fa0aF9FCF2f676840E015eD31e5d5C4 and the /verify endpoint. Alert companies are black boxes.

§ 02

What we deliberately don't claim

Honesty is the brand

  • Not faster than native StreetEasy alerts or LeaseAlert SMS. Per-request scrape ≠ realtime push. We sell breadth, structure, and composability — not latency.
  • Vision/photo filters (natural light, brick walls) are roadmap. Tested at ~$0.013/listing, not shipped yet. Mark as "next" in any comparison.
  • Rent-stabilized cross-check: RentReboot owns that. HCR rent-stabilized list + DOB/HPD/311/DOHMH city data is public; we may add it later.

We're on a TEE with receipts. If we oversell, you can audit it. These constraints keep the positioning honest.
